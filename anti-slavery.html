Modern Anti-Slavery Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Advanced Aircraft Company (“AAC”) is committed to combating modern slavery, human trafficking, forced labor, and child labor in our operations and supply chain. This policy sets out our expectations and approach.

1. Our commitment

We prohibit the use of forced, bonded, indentured, or involuntary prison labor, human trafficking, and child labor. We expect the same standards from suppliers, subcontractors, and channel partners who provide goods or services to AAC.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all AAC employees, officers, contractors, and to suppliers and partners as a condition of doing business with us, including manufacturing, component, and logistics providers supporting SPEARHEAD platforms.

3. Supply chain due diligence

  • We prefer suppliers with transparent labor practices and NDAA-aligned, traceable supply chains
  • We may require certifications, questionnaires, or contractual representations regarding labor standards
  • We reserve the right to audit or request evidence of compliance
  • High-risk geographies and categories receive enhanced scrutiny

4. Employee awareness

Relevant personnel involved in procurement and supplier management are expected to understand this policy and escalate concerns. Training may be provided as our programs mature.

5. Reporting

Anyone who suspects modern slavery or related abuses in connection with AAC operations or suppliers should report promptly to compliance@flyaac.com or via their manager. Reports may be made anonymously where permitted by law. AAC prohibits retaliation against good-faith reporters.

6. Remediation

If we identify indicators of modern slavery in our supply chain, we will take appropriate action, which may include investigation, supplier remediation plans, suspension, or termination of relationships, and cooperation with authorities.

7. Review

This policy is reviewed periodically by AAC leadership and updated as needed to reflect legal requirements and best practices.
