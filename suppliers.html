Supplier Relations

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Advanced Aircraft Company works with suppliers and manufacturing partners who share our commitment to quality, NDAA compliance, ethical labor, and on-time delivery for tactical UAS.

1. What we look for

  • NDAA & origin: Preference for U.S. and allied sources with clear traceability and lot control
  • Quality systems: AS9100, ISO 9001, or equivalent where applicable; serialization and shelf-life control for critical parts
  • Cyber & CMMC readiness: Ability to support CMMC and controlled unclassified information requirements when required
  • Ethical labor: Alignment with our Modern Anti-Slavery Policy
  • Capacity & scale: Ability to support both prototype and production volumes, including expeditionary manufacturing concepts

2. Categories of interest

  • Motors, ESCs, propellers, and other propulsion systems (NDAA-compliant)
  • Flight controllers, mission computers, and edge AI modules
  • Radios (MANET, SATCOM), antennas, and datalinks
  • EO/IR, radar, event cameras, and other sensors
  • Batteries, hybrid power generation, and power electronics
  • Airframe materials, additive manufacturing, and assembly services
  • Munitions interfaces and payload integration (authorized partners only)

3. How to engage

Qualified suppliers may introduce capabilities to suppliers@flyaac.com with:

  • Company overview, CAGE/UEI if available, and quality certifications
  • NDAA / country-of-origin statements for proposed items
  • Relevant product datasheets and lead-time / MOQ information
  • Points of contact for technical and commercial discussions

Unsolicited marketing without technical relevance may not receive a response. Existing channel partners (e.g., Chase Defense manufacturing network) remain primary production paths for many SPEARHEAD components.

4. Conduct expectations

Suppliers must not offer improper gifts or inducements, must protect AAC confidential information, and must comply with applicable export controls, sanctions, and anti-corruption laws (including FCPA).
