COMPLIANCE

NDAA · COTS · MOSA

Designed from the ground up on trusted supply chains with transparent procurement pathways, open systems, and commercial leverage.

COTS

Commercial off-the-shelf components already flying on AAC aircraft: flight controllers, batteries, servos, onboard compute, radios, and sensors. Proven parts reduce risk, cost, and lead time. New HSI design reuses 9 of 10 spearhead subsystems.

NDAA

All AAC aircraft are NDAA Sec. 848 compliant and built in the USA with qualified, traceable parts. Active path to Blue List self-certification. Our supplier roadmap includes robust and resilient NDAA alternatives for all components.

MOSA

Modular Open Systems Approach architecture. Open modular payload bays, standard interfaces, and software-defined payloads. AAC electrical engineering leadership participates in MOSA and SOSA community standards for interoperability.

How we stay compliant

  • 01 US design, manufacture, and assembly (Hampton VA · Chase manufacturing channel)
  • 02 Qualified, lot-controlled, serialized components with shelf-life and traceability
  • 03 Continuous supplier testing for NDAA-compliant motors, ESCs, radios, sensors
  • 04 Open architecture for government and third-party payload integration
  • 05 CMMC path with channel partners; UEI / CAGE registered
  • 06 Expeditionary manufacturing options that remain NDAA-aligned
