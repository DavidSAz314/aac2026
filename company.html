MISSION

Our mission

Our mission is to build the best tactical UAS systems possible to support our warfighters in any domain and environment.

Founded in 2017, AAC develops hybrid fuel-electric and high-performance electric uncrewed aircraft for defense. We combine patent-pending aerodynamics, NDAA-compliant COTS, and the SPEARHEAD contested-ops stack so units can train and fight with organic multi-mission capability.
